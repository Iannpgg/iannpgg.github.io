3D mechanical assembly modeled in SolidWorks. Click and drag inside the viewer below to rotate, pan, and inspect the geometry in full 3D.

💡 Engineering Thought Process

  • Reference & Scaling: Scaled blueprints in SolidWorks referencing base KPV machine gun dimensions to resolve conflicting web specifications.
  • Kinematics & Motion: Engineered rotational joints and gear mates for 360° traverse and elevation from -10° to +90°.
  • Material Selection: Cast alloy steel specified for structural frames and plain carbon steel for high-stress mechanisms.
